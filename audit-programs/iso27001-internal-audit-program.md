## Audit Objectives

1. Determine whether the ISMS conforms to ISO 27001:2022 requirements (Clauses 4–10)
2. Assess the effective implementation and maintenance of ISMS controls
3. Evaluate Annex A controls selected in the Statement of Applicability (SoA)
4. Identify nonconformities, observations, and opportunities for improvement
